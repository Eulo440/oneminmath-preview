Fraction practice

Fractions are where many students first get stuck in math. Short, frequent practice with instant feedback helps. In One Minute Math you type the top number and the bottom number in stacked boxes, and the answer is checked as you go.

What each level covers

  • Adding: Level 1 same denominators (2/7 + 3/7), Level 2 different denominators (1/2 + 1/4), Level 3 mixed numbers.
  • Subtracting: Level 1 same denominators, Level 2 different denominators, Level 3 mixed numbers with borrowing.
  • Multiplying: Level 1 whole × fraction (3/4 × 8), Level 2 fraction × fraction, Level 3 cancelling and mixed numbers.
  • Dividing: Level 1 by a whole number or unit fraction, Level 2 fraction ÷ fraction, Level 3 any fractions and mixed numbers.

Answers in simplest form

Answers count only in simplest form: 1/2, not 2/4. If you type an equal answer that is not simplified, the site tells you it is equal but needs simplifying, so you learn the habit without guessing what went wrong. A whole-number answer goes in the top box only.

Quick reminders

  • Adding or subtracting: make the denominators the same, then add or subtract the numerators. 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12
  • Multiplying: top × top, bottom × bottom, then simplify (or cancel first).
  • Dividing: keep, change, flip: 2/3 ÷ 1/4 = 2/3 × 4/1 = 8/3.

Fractions rely on quick multiplication facts. If finding common denominators is slow, a few times-table rounds help.
