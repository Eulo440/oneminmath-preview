Multiplication facts practice

Knowing the times tables by heart makes fractions, division, area and algebra much easier. One Minute Math drills the facts one at a time, shows you which ones you miss, and brings those back more often until they stick.

1-minute times tablesPractice to 12 × 12, no clock

Levels

  • Level 1: tables 1–5. A good start for second and third grade.
  • Level 2: tables 2–10.
  • Level 3: all tables to 12 × 12.

A sensible order to learn them

  1. 2s, 5s and 10s (patterns kids already know from counting).
  2. 3s and 4s (4s are double the 2s).
  3. 9s (up to 9 × 10, the digits of the answer add up to 9) and 11s.
  4. 6s, 7s and 8s: the hardest ones. Only a few are left once you know the others and remember that 3 × 7 is the same as 7 × 3.
  5. 12s last.
