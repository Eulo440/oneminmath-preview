Free timed math practice, nothing else

One Minute Math is a free math facts drill that works in any browser, on phones, tablets and computers. Pick an operation, a level and a kind of number, then answer as many problems as you can in 60 seconds. Answers check themselves as you type. Your bests and progress stay on your device. There are no ads, no accounts, no prizes or streaks to chase, just the math.

It is the classic “mad minute” drill from school, made for screens: whole-number facts, fractions and mixed numbers, and decimals, plus a no-clock practice mode, a round of only the questions you missed lately, and printable worksheets with answer keys.
