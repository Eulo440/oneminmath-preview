Division facts practice

Every division fact is a multiplication fact in reverse: if you know 6 × 7 = 42, you know 42 ÷ 7 = 6. Drilling division right after multiplication locks both in. All answers are whole numbers, so you can focus on the facts.

Tip: think “what times?”

For 56 ÷ 8, ask “8 times what is 56?” If that is slow, the times table for that number needs more practice first.
