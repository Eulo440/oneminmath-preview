Printable math facts worksheets

Every setting on One Minute Math can be printed as a worksheet: 60 problems on one page (40 for fractions, which need more room), with a separate answer key page. A new set of problems is made every time, so you can print as many different sheets as you like. They are free, with no watermark and no sign-up.

How to print a worksheet

  1. Open a setting below (or choose your own on the home page).
  2. Tap Print a worksheet at the top of the page.
  3. Print, or save as PDF. The sheet prints in black and white, with a line for name, date and score.

After a timed round, Print these facts makes a sheet that mixes the facts you just missed with new ones.
