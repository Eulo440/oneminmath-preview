For teachers and parents

One Minute Math is a free fluency tool with nothing in the way: no ads, no logins, no rewards to chase, and no student data. Share the link, and kids are practicing within seconds.

Open One Minute MathTimes tables, 1 minute

Ideas for the classroom

  • Warm-up: one timed round at the start of class. Students write down their score and try to beat it tomorrow.
  • Targeted practice: share a link that opens a specific setting, for example the 7s table or adding fractions with unlike denominators. Any setting on the guide pages has its own link.
  • Missed questions: the Missed questions clock option gives each student a round of only the facts they missed lately, and a fact drops off once they get it right twice in a row.
  • Paper drills: print worksheets with answer keys for days without devices.
  • Projector: run practice mode on the board and have the class answer together.

For parents and homeschoolers

Five minutes a day, a few rounds, is plenty. Kids compete with their own best score rather than other people, and the progress chart shows improvement over time. Each device keeps its own progress, so siblings can use separate devices or browsers.

Questions

Is One Minute Math really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no paid tier, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

Do students need an account or email?

No. Nobody signs in. Progress and personal bests are saved only in the browser on that device.

What data does the site collect?

No cookies and no personal data. A privacy-friendly counter (GoatCounter) records anonymous visit totals. The Hall of Fame stores only 3-letter initials and a score, and only if a player chooses to add them.

What grades is it for?

Addition and subtraction facts fit grades 1–2, times tables and division grades 3–4, fractions, mixed numbers and decimals grades 4–7, and anyone who wants to stay sharp.

Does it work on Chromebooks, iPads and phones?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser. On phones and tablets the number keyboard opens automatically.

Can I print worksheets?

Yes. Any setting prints as a worksheet with an answer key, with a fresh set of problems every time.
