Addition facts practice

Quick addition is the base for everything else in arithmetic. Start with the single-digit facts, then move up to two-digit numbers once those are automatic.

1-minute additionPractice, no clock

Levels

  • Level 1Facts to 10 + 10
  • Level 2Two-digit sums to 100
  • Level 3Two-digit + two-digit

Strategies that help

  • Make ten: 8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3 = 13.
  • Doubles and near doubles: 6 + 7 is 6 + 6, plus 1.
  • Turn it around: 2 + 9 is the same as 9 + 2. Start from the bigger number.
  • For two-digit sums, add the tens, then the ones: 47 + 28 = 60 + 15 = 75.
