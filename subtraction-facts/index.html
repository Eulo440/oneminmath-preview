Subtraction facts practice

Subtraction facts are addition facts in reverse: 13 − 5 = 8 because 5 + 8 = 13. Answers are never negative, so younger kids can practice with confidence.

1-minute subtractionPractice, no clock

Levels

  • Level 1Facts within 20
  • Level 2Within 100, answers to 50
  • Level 3Within 200

Strategies that help

  • Count up: for 12 − 9, count up from 9 to 12. That's 3.
  • Back through ten: 15 − 7 = 15 − 5 − 2 = 8.
  • Think addition: 14 − 6 asks “6 plus what is 14?”

Mix all four operations with the Mixed setting, or try subtracting mixed numbers with borrowing.
